## 2. Source Gap Analysis (Where AI Models Actually Pull Consensus)

Across the responses, AI engines relied primarily on **third-party publishers and software directories** rather than Notion's own domain:

| Cited Domain | Citation Count | Ownership | Strategic Implication |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| `zapier.com` | **6** | Third-party comparison | Primary consensus driver — target updated comparison guides & feature accuracy |
| `pcmag.com` | **4** | Editorial review | High authority for B2B validation |
| `techradar.com` | **3** | Editorial review | Frequently pulled into "best tool for..." answers |
| `notion.so` | **2** | **Owned domain** | Under-cited relative to 53% brand mention share — needs structured FAQ & pricing data |
| `g2.com` | **2** | Review directory | Critical for B2B buyer verification |
| `capterra.it` | **1** | Local IT directory | Strong local Italian signal for non-English queries |
